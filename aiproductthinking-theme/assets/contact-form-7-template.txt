<!--
  Contact Form 7 template for the AI Product Thinking theme.
  -------------------------------------------------------------
  How to use:
    1. Install and activate the "Contact Form 7" plugin.
    2. Go to: Contact > Add New (or open the existing "Contact form 1").
    3. Open the "Form" tab and replace its contents with the markup
       between the BEGIN/END markers below.
    4. Open the "Mail" tab and confirm [your-name], [your-email],
       [your-subject], [your-message] are referenced in the recipient
       template (they are by default).
    5. Save. The shortcode in page-contact.php expects ID "f7daf39"
       and title "Contact form 1" — if your form has a different ID,
       open page-contact.php and update the shortcode line.

  Note: Contact Form 7 wraps every <label> in <p> tags automatically,
  which the theme styles with .form-group spacing. The matching CSS
  block lives in style.css under "Contact Form 7 styling".
-->

==== BEGIN CF7 FORM TEMPLATE ====

<label> Your name
    [text* your-name autocomplete:name placeholder "Your name"] </label>

<label> Your email
    [email* your-email autocomplete:email placeholder "you@company.com"] </label>

<label> Subject
    [text* your-subject placeholder "What is this about?"] </label>

<label> Your message (optional)
    [textarea your-message placeholder "Tell me about your perspective on this space, your background, or what kind of conversation you'd like to have…"] </label>

[submit "Submit"]

==== END CF7 FORM TEMPLATE ====
